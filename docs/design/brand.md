# Brand — the soul

> **Design set:** [Index](./CLAUDE.md) · **Brand** · [System](./system.md)
> Personality and feel. The concrete tokens that deliver it live in system.md.

## The feeling in one line

> Calm & premium on the surface; data-dense & sharp in the depth. **Warmth lives
> in the words, not the pixels.** Dark-first.

A well-made instrument you're glad to open after a game — quiet, confident, on
your side. It earns trust by being honest and beautiful, not loud.

## Personality

| We are                                     | We are not                               |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Calm, confident, premium                   | Loud, gamified, childish                 |
| Honest — grades you against a better _you_ | Harsh — grades you against a machine     |
| Quietly intelligent; depth on demand       | Bloomberg-cold; density for its own sake |
| Spacious, restrained, one accent           | Busy, rainbow, chrome-heavy (chess.com)  |
| Warm in voice                              | Saccharine or condescending              |

## The two-layer rule

This is the whole aesthetic strategy, so it's load-bearing:

- **Surface layer** (home, game cards, recaps) — calm & premium. Generous space,
  few numbers, one accent, restrained motion. A beginner is never intimidated
  here.
- **Depth layer** (full game analysis, stats deep-dives) — data-dense & sharp.
  Tight grids, tabular figures, fine rules, the analyst craft. Stat-lovers get
  fed here; you reach it by _choosing_ to dig in.

Never invert these. Density on the surface breaks the promise; vagueness in the
depth wastes the data.

## Voice & tone

Warmth is carried entirely by language, which lets the visuals stay cool.

- **Plain over jargon.** "You were winning here" beats "+3.2 eval at move 18."
  The number can be _available_, but the sentence leads.
- **On your side, never scolding.** "You had this — here's where it slipped," not
  "Blunder. -4.1."
- **Honest, not flattering.** We don't fake praise. We point at the _real_ chance
  you missed (human-relevant, not engine-only) and respect that you can handle it.
- **Calm confidence.** Short sentences. No exclamation-mark confetti. The product
  is sure of itself.
- **Beginner-safe, never condescending.** Assume curiosity and intelligence, not
  knowledge. Explain the term the first time; don't talk down.

Quick gut-check copy:

- ✅ "Clearly winning for 4 moves — then this gave it back."
- ✅ "Your sharpest game this week."
- ❌ "Oops! Big blunder! 😬 -4.1"
- ❌ "Suboptimal continuation; principled refutation available."

## Motion

Motion means something or it doesn't happen. Smooth, quick, eased (see system.md
for tokens). Reserve anything celebratory for genuinely earned moments (a real
breakthrough, a milestone) — and even then, keep it tasteful. The default is
near-silent.
