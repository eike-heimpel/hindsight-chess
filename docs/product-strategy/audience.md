## Beachhead: beginner-first (≈500–900)

We plant the flag on the beginner band — and specifically build for the player
**Eike is** (currently 500–700), so the core loop gets dogfooded ruthlessly.

Why this band:

- It's the **mode** of the entire rating distribution — the largest population in
  chess.
- Games here are decided by **blunders, hung pieces, missed mate-in-1, and blown
  winning positions** — not subtle positional drift. The features we love
  (blunder run-through, winnable losses) are tailor-made for exactly this.
- Highest **emotional resonance**: "I was up a queen and lost" is the universal
  beginner experience. That feeling is our hook.
- Perfect **taste alignment** — we are the user.

## Principle: segment by the moment, not the rating

A rating band is not a market. "Everyone under 1500" is ~85–90% of all players —
that's _no one_ in particular. What separates a buyer from a non-buyer is
**intent**, not skill. Two 700s: one shrugs and clicks New Game (casual); one sits
there asking "why do I keep doing this?" (motivated). Both are welcome — they just
enter the funnel differently.

## The funnel

- **Casual majority → free.** The home + per-game story. Identity, streaks, fun
  shareable stats. Costs ~nothing (pure aggregation, no LLM). This is the wide,
  viral top — and the habit that brings the motivated subset back.
- **Motivated subset → paid.** The depth: worst-first blunder drilling, winnable-
  loss analysis, plain-English explanations. They pay for getting better.

Most users never convert, and that's fine — same as Strava. The free home is the
acquisition engine, not a loss leader to apologize for.

## Architected to span the climb (500 → ~1500)

Market and _tune_ to 500–900 (our taste), but build the home/stats spine to span
the whole beginner→intermediate arc so the product doesn't break as a user
improves. Depth scales up quietly; the home stays universal. Don't put a rating
number in the marketing — sell the feeling ("see how you really played"), not a
bracket.

## Willingness-to-pay reality (honest)

- The beginner band has the **lowest WTP and highest churn** in chess (people
  quit, or quit being nagged). We accept this — monetization leans on delight and
  identity, with light coaching for the motivated, not heavy study.
- Anchors for when we price: chess.com Diamond ~$100/yr, Aimchess ~$10–15/mo,
  DecodeChess ~$10/mo, Chessable courses $50–200. The $5–12/mo band is
  established; we won't be inventing a price expectation.
- Math that matters: at ~$8/mo, **1,000 paying users ≈ $96k/yr** — a real
  lifestyle business off a rounding error of the market. We do not need to beat
  chess.com to win.

## Ambition

Amazing, beloved core loop **first**. Small revenue **second**. Anything bigger —
including a chess.com-style acquisition (a genuinely good outcome) — is upside
that _follows_ from people loving the product, never the thing we optimize for.
